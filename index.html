Choose a game to see each wager, its qualifying hands, and its complete payout table.

“To one” means winnings. A $5 wager at 3:1 pays $15 in winnings. The player also keeps the $5 wager: $20 total.

Three of a kind ≠ three pairs. In UTH, three of a kind pays 3:1 on Trips. If seven cards contain three pairs, the best five-card hand is two pair; the Trips wager loses.
